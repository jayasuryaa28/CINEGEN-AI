# CINEGEN AI – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

---

### Q1: Is Python required to run CINEGEN AI?
**No.** CINEGEN AI is built entirely in TypeScript and JavaScript. Neural models run natively inside Node.js via `@tensorflow/tfjs`. You do not need Python, Conda, or Python virtual environments.

### Q2: Does CINEGEN AI send my video files to external AI services?
**No.** Your videos are processed 100% locally on your computer (Mode B) or on your private cloud instance (Mode A). CINEGEN AI never sends media to external proprietary APIs (like OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Replicate).

### Q3: What video and audio formats are supported?
- **Containers**: MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM.
- **Video Codecs**: H.264 (AVC), H.265 (HEVC), VP9, ProRes.
- **Audio Codecs**: AAC, MP3, AC3, FLAC, PCM.
- **Output Container**: High-compatibility MP4 (H.264 video + 48kHz AAC dual-audio).

### Q4: Why are ECHONET and VOICENET bypassed by default?
During our Phase 5.1 and Phase 8 empirical evaluations on held-out test data, ECHONET (dereverberation) and VOICENET (vocal boost) produced sub-threshold SDR gains on real recordings. In adherence to our strict quality principles, both models remain bypassed by default to prevent any risk of acoustic distortion reaching your final export.

### Q5: How does the Dual-Audio feature work?
Every restored video generated by CINEGEN AI contains two audio tracks:
- **Track 1**: Restored dialogue and enhanced audio processed through NOISENET and STEMNET.
- **Track 2**: Original unaltered audio from your source recording.
You can toggle between tracks in any media player (such as VLC) for instant A/B comparison.

### Q6: Can I process long feature-length movies (e.g. 2 to 4 hours)?
**Yes.** CINEGEN AI divides long videos into integer-frame 10-second chunks and maintains atomic checkpoint state. If processing is interrupted, you can resume seamlessly from the last completed chunk without re-processing earlier segments.
